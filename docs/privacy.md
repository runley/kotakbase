# Kotakbase Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Kotakbase is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It has no accounts of its own, no servers of its own, and no analytics or tracking. This page explains what the app stores and which outside services it talks to.

## What stays on your computer

Your watch progress, Your List, ratings, history, settings, installed extensions, downloads and Watch Together name are stored only on your computer, in Kotakbase's app data folder and your downloads folder. Nobody, including the Kotakbase developers, receives them. Uninstalling Kotakbase or deleting that folder removes them.

If you sign in with AniList, your AniList access token is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows' own data protection. It is used only to talk to AniList.

## Services Kotakbase talks to

Kotakbase only contacts the services below, and only to do what's described. Each has its own privacy policy.

- **AniList** (anilist.co): show details, schedules and search. If you sign in, your progress, list and ratings are read from and written to your AniList account.
- **MyAnimeList** (myanimelist.net): show details when AniList is unavailable.
- **ani.zip** and **AniSkip** (aniskip.com): episode details, and opening/ending times for the "Skip Intro" button.
- **Shikimori** (shikimori.io), only when Kotakbase is in Russian or Kazakh: Russian show titles and descriptions, looked up by the shows' MyAnimeList ids.
- **Discord**, if the Discord desktop app is running and the setting is on: the title, episode, cover image and playback position of what you're watching are shared with Discord to show on your profile. Turn it off in Settings → Discord.
- **GitHub** (github.com): checking for and downloading Kotakbase updates. This website and its invite pages are also hosted by GitHub Pages; an invite link's room code stays in your browser and isn't sent to GitHub.
- **Google Fonts**: the fonts the app's pages use.
- **Extensions** you install: their code runs in Kotakbase and contacts the sites it was written for when you search for a source. Extensions are made by third parties; Kotakbase doesn't control what they contact.
- **BitTorrent and WebTorrent**: when you stream or download, your IP address is visible to torrent trackers and to other people sharing the same torrent. This is how BitTorrent works.
- **Watch Together**: rooms find each other through public WebTorrent trackers and connect directly between devices (with Google and Twilio STUN servers helping devices find a route to each other). Your IP address is visible to the trackers and to the people in your room. Your chosen name, chat messages and what the room is watching are sent only to the people in the room.

## Children

Kotakbase isn't directed at children under 13.
