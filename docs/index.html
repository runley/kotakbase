Free and open source

Anime, playing the moment you press play.

Kotakbase finds a release, streams it straight from the torrent and keeps your AniList up to date. Read manga in the same app, on your PC or your Android phone. No waiting for downloads, no accounts, no ads.

Everything for watching anime

Streams right away

Episodes play while they download, and the next one is ready before this one ends.

AniList sync

Sign in once and your progress, list and ratings update as you watch.

Watch Together

Share an invite link and watch in sync with friends, with a chat beside the video.

Offline downloads

Download an episode or a whole season and watch it later without the internet.

Skip intros

One click past the opening and ending, from the file's chapters or AniSkip.

Proper subtitles

Styled signs and songs with the release's own fonts, and mpv for Dolby and DTS audio.

Your choice of sources

Works with Hayase-compatible extensions that you choose and install.

Schedule and history

See what airs this week and pick up exactly where you left off.

Discord status

Optionally show friends what you're watching, right on your Discord profile.

Manga, too

One switch away

Tap the logo and Kotakbase turns into mangalbase, with AniList's manga, your manga list and your reading history.

A reader that fits

Read as one long vertical strip for webtoons, or page by page, right to left or left to right.

Progress that keeps up

Reaching the end of a chapter marks it read and updates your AniList. Chapters come from manga extensions you install.

On Android, folded or not

Made for foldables

Unfolded, a show opens beside the page you're on and the reader shows two pages at once. Fold it and everything moves to one screen, even mid-episode.

The same app

Streaming, manga, AniList sync, downloads and Watch Together with friends on PC. Plus picture-in-picture and a tabletop mode when it's half folded.

Any Android phone

Works on regular phones too, Android 8 or newer. Native and light on memory, with hardware video decoding.
